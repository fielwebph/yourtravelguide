Booking and Pricing

Rate Inclusions

Your agreed rate includes vehicle fuel, road tolls, and driver fees. Admission tickets to attractions, meals, and personal expenses are strictly excluded from the package price.

Service Duration

Day tours are fixed at a standard 8 to 10 hours as specified in your confirmation. These hours are continuous and cannot be shortened, split, or moved to a different time slot on the day of the tour.

Vehicle Assignment

Our vehicle models, makes, or colors may vary based on daily availability. While we guarantee the passenger capacity you booked, the specific vehicle assigned may differ from the sample photos.

Quote Validity

For private cars, price quotes provided by our booking agents remain valid for exactly 3 days or 72 hours from the date and time of issue. Rates are subject to change without prior notice once the validity window expires. To lock in your quoted rate and guarantee vehicle availability, full booking confirmation and payment must be completed before the 72-hour period ends.

Surcharges

After-Hours Surcharge (22:00–07:00)

Transfers or tour services scheduled between 22:00 and 07:00 are subject to a JPY 2,000 after-hours surcharge.

For tours that extend beyond the agreed end time, additional hourly overtime charges also apply. These charges are separate from the after-hours surcharge and may vary depending on the vehicle and service booked.

Please plan your schedule accordingly to avoid unexpected additional charges.

Overtime Fees

In case you're running late or behind your tour schedule, overtime charges will apply once your agreed service period has ended. Overtime is charged per hour based on the vehicle booked:

  • 7 & 10-seater: JPY 5,000 per hour
  • 14-seater: JPY 6,000 per hour
  • 22-seater: JPY 10,000 per hour

Please keep your itinerary within the confirmed service hours to avoid additional charges.

Cleaning Fees

Please help us keep the vehicle clean and ready for the next guests. Mess-related cleaning fees start at JPY 35,000 and may increase depending on the extent of the mess and the vehicle's condition.

These charges may apply to spills, stains, excessive rubbish, food or drink residue, urine, and any other issues that require professional deep cleaning and vehicle downtime.

Service Policies

Airport Pick-up Free Waiting Time

Your driver will wait up to 90 minutes after your scheduled landing time at no extra charge. After the complimentary waiting period ends, additional waiting time will be subject to vehicle-specific overtime charges.

To avoid extra charges, please make sure you are ready to meet your driver within the allotted waiting time. Delays caused by immigration, baggage collection, or other airport procedures are included in the waiting period.

City Transfer Free Waiting Time

A 30-minute complimentary waiting period is provided for city transfers. Once the grace period has ended, additional waiting charges will apply based on the vehicle and service booked.

To avoid extra charges, we recommend being at the pick-up point a few minutes before your scheduled time.

Meet and Greet

For small cars, our Meet & Greet service is available for an additional JPY 2,000.

Your driver will meet you at the designated airport meeting point with a personalized name sign, making it easier to spot your ride and get your trip started without the guesswork.

Capacity Compliance

It is the client's strict responsibility to check the maximum passenger and luggage limits of their selected vehicle prior to finalizing their booking. For safety and legal reasons, the driver reserves the right to deny service if the actual number of passengers or luggage items exceeds the vehicle's legal and structural capacity. In the event that service cannot be rendered or is disrupted due to capacity limits being exceeded, no refunds, partial credits, or compensation will be issued.

Communication

A dedicated WhatsApp group including you, our support team, and the driver will be created the night before your scheduled trip. This ensures seamless coordination and real-time updates for your peace of mind.

Traffic and Itinerary Adjustments

While our drivers utilize real-time navigation to choose the best routes possible, YTG is not liable for delays caused by heavy traffic, road accidents, or seasonal road closures. No refunds or extensions will be given for itinerary locations missed due to traffic delays beyond our control.

Route Modifications

Sudden changes to the confirmed itinerary on the day of the tour are not allowed without explicit approval from our booking office, as they may conflict with driving hour limits or scheduled routes.
